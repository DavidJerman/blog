Another great place I've travelled to was Tsukuba. Tsukuba is a city a bit out of Tokyo, about an hour long train ride out of center of Tokyo. It is a city known for it's universities. One of my coworkers who is fascinated with humanoid robots and art. And also one of my friends I met later that day also studies in Tsukuba. The point is, this place is filled with universities and research places. And I decided to visit one of those: JAXA. JAXA stands for Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency and I visited the Tsukuba Space Center. This was my first time visiting a space agency of any kind so I was really excited.

![Tsukuba JAXA entrance](/blog/media/travel/tsukuba/jaxa_entrance.jpg)

The space center was quite far away from my apartment though. I first had to take the Tsukuba Express train to get to Tsukuba, and after that a local bus to get to the space center. Luckily the bus dropped me right in front of the entrance!
